# Yard Card

Might and Power Club — best presented horse, Caulfield Saturday 19 September 2026.

**Open on your phone:** https://psaundry.github.io/yard-card/

Works in iPhone Chrome and Safari. One phone per table.

1. Tap **This is us** on your race.
2. Score one horse at a time.
3. Present as they walk to the start (leave at T-20).

Host pin: `mpc19`.
