# Yard Card — Might and Power Club

Private club app for Underwood Stakes Day, Caulfield, Saturday 19 September 2026.

**Club link (after Pages is on):** https://psaundry.github.io/yard-card/

This is not a public product. Do not submit it to search engines or racing forums.

## First-time Pages switch (needed once)

1. Open https://github.com/psaundry/yard-card/settings/pages
2. Source: **Deploy from a branch**
3. Branch: **main** / **/** (root)
4. Save. Wait 1–2 minutes.

Fallback while Pages builds: open `index.html` from this repo on your phone, or AirDrop the file.

## How tables use it

- One phone per table.
- Tap **This is us** on your race.
- Score horses in the mounting yard (about 10–15 minutes).
- Award as they walk to the start.
- **Back to tables** shows every race again. Official allocations stay put.

Race 1 is **The Taggart** at 12:10. Leave-suite times are T-20.

Host PIN: `mpc19`

Scores are saved on that phone. The Board tab on the same phone shows the winner. Another phone will not see those marks unless you score on it too.
